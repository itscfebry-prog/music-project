# Fluid Frequency Harmony Slider

A dynamic microtonal harmony explorer and frequency generator designed to break the barriers of standard Western tuning systems. This project allows users to seamlessly navigate frequencies and explore diverse musical microtonal scales.

Coding music across a wide range—from traditional to microtonal and new harmonies.

## Features

- Fluid Frequency Manipulation: Smoothly shift between custom frequencies starting from 20 Hz.
- Microtonal Tuning Exploration: Instantly switch between multiple EDO (Equal Divisions of the Octave) and traditional scales:
- Standard & Traditional: 5 EDO (Slendro Pentatonic), 12 EDO (Standard Equal Temperament).
- Symmetrical Systems: 2 EDO (Tritone), 3 EDO (Augmented), 4 EDO (Diminished), 6 EDO (Whole Tone), 8 EDO (Octatonic).
- Microtonal Shifting: 7 EDO (Equiheptatonic), 9 EDO, 10 EDO, and 11 EDO (Neutral Microtonal).
- Flexible Snap Engine: Toggle between precise absolute snapping or light alignment to maintain organic pitch behaviors.

## Tech Stack

- Audio Engine: Web Audio API (or your specific audio framework)
- Interface: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
